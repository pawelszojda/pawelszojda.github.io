Enterprise · NDA

B2B medical distribution platform

  1. OfferPrice thresholds
  2. ApprovalRole-based chain
  3. OrderState managed
  4. Subiekt ERPAuto dispatch

Context

Medical-products distributor managing commercial workflows across internal sales teams and external client companies, with a hard dependency on ERP synchronization and public tender compliance.

Problem

Tender processing was entirely spreadsheet-driven with no validation layer. Offer approval chains had no systematic tracking. ERP order dispatch required manual coordinator intervention on every transfer.

Solution

Helped build a multi-role Rails monolith covering the full commercial lifecycle - offer creation with price-threshold approval flows, order state management, automated Subiekt ERP dispatch, and an async AI pipeline for spreadsheet normalization via OpenAI.

Outcome

Full order lifecycle automated from offer to ERP dispatch with zero manual handoffs. Tender spreadsheet normalization reduced from hours to minutes. Role-based access enforced across 8+ distinct user roles spanning internal staff and client organizations.
